    Interface ConfigRegistryExperimental

    Read-only view of every per-contract LB4 config the engine has loaded.

    Configs come from configs/*.config.json files validated against the engine-generated _meta/model-config.schema.json at pipeline stage 5. The registry surfaces them keyed by $contractId (which matches the referenced schema's $id).

    Marked optional on EmitterContext.configs so emitters that don't touch LB4 config (most sidecar emitters) don't need to care it exists.
