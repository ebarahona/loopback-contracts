    Interface LoopbackConfigJson

    Top-level shape of loopback.config.json — the per-project entry point the lb-contracts init command writes and lb-contracts gen reads on every run.

    Bound under ContractsBindings.CONFIG — exposed as @public because the binding key is @public and the generic on the BindingKey is part of the public contract.
